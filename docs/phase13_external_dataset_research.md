# InspectAI – Phase 13: External Bearing Dataset Research & Provenance Audit

**Date**: November 2026  
**Auditor**: InspectAI Release & Validation Engineering  
**Objective**: Comprehensive investigation into independent public bearing datasets to establish an empirical cross-dataset validation pipeline beyond the Case Western Reserve University (CWRU) test rig.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Condition monitoring models trained exclusively on a single laboratory test rig frequently memorize rig-specific acoustic dynamics, boundary resonance modes, and idealized fault geometries. To evaluate the true physical transferability of InspectAI's frozen candidate model (`SpectralFusionCNN`), we conducted an institutional audit of five recognized benchmark bearing datasets:

1. **Paderborn University (PU) Bearing Dataset** (Chair of Design and Drive Technology, Germany)
2. **MFPT Bearing Fault Dataset** (Society for Machinery Failure Prevention Technology, USA)
3. **Southeast University (SEU) Bearing Dataset** (Drivetrain Dynamic Simulator, China)
4. **Jiangnan University (JNU) Bearing Dataset** (Key Laboratory of Advanced Process Control, China)
5. **University of Cincinnati (IMS) Bearing Dataset** (Center for Intelligent Maintenance Systems / NASA, USA)

Each dataset was evaluated on institutional provenance, legal licensing, bearing geometries, sampling rates, sensor placements, condition labels, and physical compatibility with InspectAI's 12 kHz / 2,048-sample dual-branch architecture.

## 2. In-Depth Candidate Dataset Investigations

### 2.1 Paderborn University (PU) Bearing Dataset

- **Official Source**: [Paderborn University KAt-DataCenter](https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/kat/forschung/bearing-datacenter)  
  *Direct Repository Index*: `https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/kat/BearingDataCenter`
- **Owner & Institution**: Chair of Design and Drive Technology (*Konstruktions- und Antriebstechnik – KAt*), Department of Mechanical Engineering, Paderborn University, Germany.  
  *Principal Investigators*: Christian Lessmeier, J. K. Kimotho, D. Zimmer, Walter Sextro (2016).
- **License & Legal Permissibility**: **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)**.  
  *Permitted Usage*: Free for academic research, education, benchmarking, and portfolio demonstration with appropriate attribution.
- **Experimental Rig Setup**:
  - Test rig comprises an electric drive motor (synchronous servo motor, 425 W), torque measurement shaft, bearing test module with interchangeable test bearings, flywheel, and load induction motor.
  - Radial load applied hydraulically (up to 1,000 N); torque load applied by load motor (up to 0.7 Nm).
- **Bearing Specifications**:
  - Bearing Model: **SKF 6203** (Deep-groove ball bearing).
  - Pitch Diameter ($D_p$): $28.5\text{ mm}$ (1.122 in).
  - Ball Diameter ($d$): $6.75\text{ mm}$ (0.266 in).
  - Number of Rolling Elements ($Z$): $8$ balls.
  - Contact Angle ($\alpha$): $0^\circ$.
- **Bearings & Recording Count**:
  - **32 Total Bearings** across three damage classifications:
    1. *Undamaged (Healthy Reference)*: 6 bearings (`K001` to `K006`).
    2. *Artificial Damage (Seeded)*: 12 bearings with discrete flaws generated by electric discharge machining (EDM, 0.25 mm trench), drilling (0.9–3.0 mm pit), or manual engraving:
       - Outer Ring: `KA04`, `KA15`, `KA16`, `KA22`, `KA30`.
       - Inner Ring: `KA01`, `KA03`, `KA05`, `KA07`, `KA08`, `KA09`.
    3. *Accelerated Lifetime Run-to-Failure (Fatigue Damage)*: 14 bearings subjected to accelerated lifetime fatigue testing:
       - Inner Ring Spalling: `KI01`, `KI03`, `KI04`, `KI05`, `KI07`, `KI08`.
       - Outer Ring Spalling: `KO01`.
       - Rolling Element / Ball Damage: `KB23`, `KB24`, `KB27` (featuring pitting and flaking on rolling elements).
- **Sensors & Sampling Rate**:
  - Accelerometer: Piezoelectric accelerometer (PCB Model 352A60) mounted vertically on the top of the test bearing adapter housing.
  - Nominal Sampling Rate: **64,000 Hz** ($64\text{ kHz}$).
  - Additional channels: Motor phase current (2 channels at 64 kHz).
- **Operating Conditions**:
  - 4 canonical operating states (20 measurements of 4.0 seconds each per condition):
    1. `N15_M07_F10`: $1500\text{ RPM}$ ($25\text{ Hz}$ shaft), $0.7\text{ Nm}$ torque, $1,000\text{ N}$ radial force.
    2. `N09_M07_F10`: $900\text{ RPM}$ ($15\text{ Hz}$ shaft), $0.7\text{ Nm}$ torque, $1,000\text{ N}$ radial force.
    3. `N15_M01_F10`: $1500\text{ RPM}$ ($25\text{ Hz}$ shaft), $0.1\text{ Nm}$ torque, $1,000\text{ N}$ radial force.
    4. `N15_M07_F04`: $1500\text{ RPM}$ ($25\text{ Hz}$ shaft), $0.7\text{ Nm}$ torque, $400\text{ N}$ radial force.
- **Signal Units & File Format**:
  - Units: Acceleration in $\text{m/s}^2$ (standard SI); easily scaled to physical $g$ ($1g = 9.80665\text{ m/s}^2$).
  - Format: MATLAB `.mat` files structured within bearing-specific `.rar` archives (e.g. `K001.rar`, `KA01.rar`, `KB23.rar`).
- **Archive Size**: ~40 GB complete dataset; individual bearing archives are ~15–25 MB each, allowing modular evaluation of selected bearings.

### 2.2 MFPT (Society for Machinery Failure Prevention Technology) Dataset

- **Official Source**: [MFPT Fault Data Sets](https://www.mfpt.org/fault-data-sets/)  
  *Archival Mirror*: [Figshare DOI: 10.6084/m9.figshare.28606802](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28606802)
- **Owner & Institution**: Society for Machinery Failure Prevention Technology (MFPT); compiled by Dr. Eric Bechhoefer.
- **License & Legal Permissibility**: Open access research use (CC BY 4.0 via Figshare).
- **Experimental Rig Setup**:
  - Benchtop bearing test rig featuring a single bearing driven by an electric motor with radial loading applied via a spring tension mechanism.
- **Bearing Specifications**:
  - Bearing Model: **Nice Bearing #1012** (radial unground ball bearing).
  - Roller Diameter ($d$): $0.235\text{ in}$ ($5.969\text{ mm}$).
  - Pitch Diameter ($D_p$): $1.245\text{ in}$ ($31.623\text{ mm}$).
  - Number of Rollers ($Z$): $8$ balls.
  - Contact Angle ($\alpha$): $0^\circ$.
- **Bearings & Recording Count**:
  - **17 Controlled Benchmark Files**:
    1. *Baseline Conditions (Healthy)*: 3 files (`baseline_1.mat`, `baseline_2.mat`, `baseline_3.mat`) recorded at $270\text{ lbs}$ load, $1000\text{ RPM}$ ($16.67\text{ Hz}$ shaft).
    2. *Outer Race Fault Conditions*: 7 files (3 files at $270\text{ lbs}$ constant load; 4 files under variable loads: $25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300\text{ lbs}$).
    3. *Inner Race Fault Conditions*: 7 files under variable loads ($0, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300\text{ lbs}$).
    4. *Ball / Rolling Element Fault*: **STRICTLY MISSING**. The MFPT benchmark collection does not include a dedicated rolling element fault class.
- **Sensors & Sampling Rate**:
  - Accelerometer mounted on the bearing housing.
  - Sampling Rate: **97,656 Hz** for baseline data (6 seconds / 585,936 samples); **48,828 Hz** for fault data (3 seconds / 146,484 samples).
- **Signal Units & File Format**:
  - Acceleration recorded in standard gravitational units ($g$).
  - MATLAB binary format (`.mat`) with header metadata structures (`load`, `rate`, `bearing`).
- **Archive Size**: ~20 MB total (exceptionally compact and easily distributed).

### 2.3 Southeast University (SEU) Bearing Dataset

- **Official Source**: [cathysiyu/Mechanical-datasets (GitHub)](https://github.com/cathysiyu/Mechanical-datasets)
- **Owner & Institution**: School of Mechanical Engineering, Southeast University (SEU), Nanjing, China; Prof. Ruqiang Yan, Dr. Siyu Shao (2018).
- **License & Legal Permissibility**: Open access for academic research (MIT License on GitHub repository).
- **Experimental Rig Setup**:
  - SpectraQuest Drivetrain Dynamic Simulator (DDS). The rig contains an induction motor, parallel gearbox, planetary gearbox, and a magnetic brake.
- **Bearing Specifications**:
  - ER-16K deep-groove ball bearing mounted on the motor drive shaft.
- **Bearings & Recording Count**:
  - 5 Health Conditions across 2 operating states:
    1. Healthy (Normal)
    2. Ball Fault
    3. Inner Race Fault
    4. Outer Race Fault
    5. Compound Fault (Inner + Outer)
  - Operating Points: $20\text{ Hz} - 0\text{ V}$ ($1200\text{ RPM}$, 0 load) and $30\text{ Hz} - 2\text{ V}$ ($1800\text{ RPM}$, load 1).
- **Sensors & Sampling Rate**:
  - 8 acquisition channels: Motor vibration, planetary gearbox vibration, parallel gearbox 3-axis vibration, motor phase current, torque, and speed.
  - Sampling Rate: **5,120 Hz** ($5.12\text{ kHz}$).
- **Signal Units & File Format**:
  - Voltage/acceleration text files (`.csv` / `.txt`).
- **Incompatibility Note**: Sampling frequency of 5,120 Hz produces a Nyquist frequency of only 2,560 Hz, strictly below InspectAI's 12 kHz input requirement (see Section 3).

### 2.4 Jiangnan University (JNU) Bearing Dataset

- **Official Source**: Key Laboratory of Advanced Process Control for Light Industry, Jiangnan University, Wuxi, China.
- **Owner & Institution**: Jiangnan University (Prof. Z. Chen et al., 2015).
- **License & Legal Permissibility**: Unspecified / Academic fair use; no formal Creative Commons license on original departmental servers; widely shared via third-party GitHub and Google Drive mirrors.
- **Experimental Rig Setup**:
  - Centrifugal fan bearing test stand.
- **Bearing Specifications**:
  - Deep-groove ball bearing (type unspecified in most mirrored distributions).
- **Bearings & Recording Count**:
  - 4 Conditions: Healthy, Inner Race Fault, Outer Race Fault, Rolling Element Fault.
  - Rotational Speeds: $600\text{ RPM}$, $800\text{ RPM}$, $1000\text{ RPM}$.
- **Sensors & Sampling Rate**:
  - PCB MA352A60 accelerometer in vertical position.
  - Sampling Rate: **50,000 Hz** ($50\text{ kHz}$).
- **Incompatibility Note**: Official institutional hosting is intermittent/broken; lack of clear licensing or permanent DOI makes formal auditing precarious.

---

### 2.5 University of Cincinnati (IMS) Bearing Dataset

- **Official Source**: NASA Prognostics Data Repository / University of Cincinnati IMS Center (Qiu et al., 2006).
- **Owner & Institution**: Center for Intelligent Maintenance Systems (IMS), University of Cincinnati.
- **License & Legal Permissibility**: NASA Open Data / Public Domain.
- **Experimental Rig Setup**:
  - Four Rexnord ZA-2115 double-row bearings installed on a continuous shaft rotating at $2,000\text{ RPM}$ under a $6,000\text{ lbs}$ static radial load.
- **Failure Types**:
  - Run-to-failure continuous lifetime records (snapshots of 20,480 samples taken every 10 or 20 minutes across 7 to 35 days).
  - Test 1: Inner race fault in bearing 3, roller element defect in bearing 4.
  - Test 2: Outer race fault in bearing 1.
  - Test 3: Outer race fault in bearing 3.
- **Incompatibility Note**: IMS is an uncurated continuous degradation trajectory designed for Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation. It lacks discrete, isolated steady-state defect geometries and contains complex multi-bearing compound degradation, making it ill-suited for 4-class discrete fault classification.

## 4. Key Takeaways & Initial Screening

1. **Southeast University (SEU) is strictly disqualified**: With a 5,120 Hz sampling rate, its Nyquist frequency is 2,560 Hz. Upsampling or zero-padding to 12 kHz creates artificial spectral voids above 2.56 kHz, invalidating the Hilbert envelope spectrum branch ($0 - 6,000\text{ Hz}$).
2. **Jiangnan University (JNU) is disqualified**: Unstable institutional hosting and lack of explicit open licensing make automated, reproducible pipeline distribution legally and technically fragile.
3. **University of Cincinnati (IMS) is disqualified**: Continuous run-to-failure fatigue spalling across four simultaneous bearings violates the single-bearing steady-state classification assumption.
4. **Paderborn University (PU) and MFPT emerge as the two viable candidates**:
   - **Paderborn University (PU)** is the strongest candidate for comprehensive 4-class evaluation, possessing an official CC BY-NC 4.0 license, SKF 6203 geometry close to CWRU's SKF 6205, 1500 RPM speed, and both artificial and real fatigue damages.
   - **MFPT** is the strongest secondary candidate for lightweight 3-class restricted evaluation (Normal, Inner Race, Outer Race).
